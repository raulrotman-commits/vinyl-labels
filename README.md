# Vinyl labels

Labels for your records: fill in the tracks (type them, paste a shop's list, or photograph the sleeve in the iPhone app), measure BPM with the microphone, preview the label on the sleeve or the record, and print it on A4 or a 62 mm label printer.

Works in the browser on phones and computers, and keeps working offline after the first visit. Your records are stored only in your own browser; use Backup to move them between devices.
