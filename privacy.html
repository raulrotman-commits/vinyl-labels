WaxTag · Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

In short

WaxTag does not collect, send or share any information about you. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What stays on your device

  • Your records, tracks, notes and settings are saved in the app's storage on your device only. We have no server and never receive them.
  • The microphone is used only while you measure BPM. The sound is analysed on the device to find the tempo, and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • The camera and your photos (iPhone app) are used only when you choose to read a track list from a photo. The text is recognised on the device, and the photo is neither kept nor sent.
  • Backups are files that you create and choose where to save or send. We never see them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party services receive data from the app.

The QR code on a label links to a record shop page that you entered yourself. Opening it takes you to that website, which has its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.

מדיניות פרטיות בקצרה

WaxTag לא אוספת, לא שולחת ולא משתפת שום מידע עליך. כל מה שאתה מזין נשמר רק במכשיר שלך. המיקרופון משמש רק למדידת BPM, והצליל מנותח במכשיר בלי להיות מוקלט או נשמר. המצלמה והתמונות משמשות רק לקריאת רשימת טראקים, והתמונה לא נשמרת ולא נשלחת. אין חשבונות, אין מעקב ואין פרסומות.
